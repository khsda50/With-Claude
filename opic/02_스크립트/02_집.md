# 집 · 거주지 (+ 집에서 보내는 휴가)

거주 문항은 **거의 반드시 나온다.** 묘사 콤보의 대표 주자다.
서베이의 `집에서 보내는 휴가` 도 이 스크립트 하나로 같이 커버한다.

---

## 내가 준 재료

| 항목 | 내용 |
|---|---|
| 형태 | 아파트, 방 3개, 부모님과 거주 |
| 거주 기간 | **25년 전 같은 동네에서 이사 → 지금까지 같은 집** |
| 현관 | 열면 바로 거실 |
| 내 방 | PS5 게임 · 컴퓨터로 일·서핑 · TV로 넷플릭스 |
| 창밖 | 건너편 아파트만 보임. 햇빛은 잘 듦 |
| 평일 밤 | 11시 귀가 → **주식 본다** |
| 주말 | 책 읽거나 넷플릭스 |
| 집안일 | 주말 분리수거 |
| 사건 | **집 내부 수리(리모델링) — 새 집으로 이사 온 것 같았다** |
| 비교 | 집이 더 넓어졌다 |

> **리모델링 시점은 "5년 전"으로 통일한다.** (시험은 사실 확인 자리가 아니다.
> 다만 답변마다 시점이 흔들리면 어색하니 **5년 전으로 고정**한다.)

---

## Q1. 사는 집을 묘사해 주세요 (묘사)

> *"Tell me about the place you live. What does it look like?"*

```
Sure. I live in an apartment with my parents. It's a three-bedroom place, and
honestly, I've lived there for a really long time. We moved in when I was in
elementary school, so it's been about twenty-five years in the same apartment.

When you walk in, the first thing you see is the living room. That's where my
parents spend most of their time, and the kitchen is right next to it.

But the room I actually care about is mine. It's basically my whole world in one
room. I have a desk with my computer, where I work and just browse the internet.
There's a TV, so I watch Netflix in there. And I have a PlayStation, which is
probably where a lot of my weekend goes.

The view isn't anything special, I have to admit. My window faces the apartment
building across from us, so that's the view — another building. But the light is
really good. In the afternoon, the room gets bright without me turning anything
on, and that's honestly the thing I like most about it.

So it's not a fancy place. But when you've been somewhere for twenty-five years,
it kind of stops being just a house.
```

**점수를 버는 지점**
- `We moved in when I was in elementary school, so it's been about twenty-five years` → **숫자 + 개인사.** 채점자 귀에 남는다
- `When you walk in, the first thing you see is…` → 공간 묘사의 표준 시작
- `The view isn't anything special, I have to admit.` → **솔직한 인정.** 완벽한 집을 묘사하는 답변보다 훨씬 사람처럼 들린다
- `But the light is really good.` → **단점 → 장점 전환.** 대비 구조
- `it kind of stops being just a house` → 마무리 통찰. **묘사를 감정으로 닫는다**

## Q2. 집에서 뭘 하나요 (습관 + `집에서 보내는 휴가`)

> *"What do you usually do at home?"*
> *"Tell me what you do when you have a vacation at home."*

```
It depends a lot on the day, because my schedule is pretty extreme right now.

On weekdays, I usually don't get home until around eleven at night, because of
work and the commute. At that point I'm too tired to do anything real. So what I
actually do is sit down and check the stock market for a while. I don't even
know why — it's not like I'm going to trade at eleven p.m. It's just become a
habit at this point. And then I go to sleep.

Weekends are completely different, and they're mostly about staying in. I read,
or I watch something on Netflix, or I play games. Sometimes I'll be in my room
for most of the day, which sounds a bit sad, but that's actually how I recover.

The one thing I have to do is take out the recycling. That's my job in the
house, every weekend. It's not exactly a huge contribution, but my mother would
notice immediately if I skipped it.

And honestly, when I get time off from work, this is pretty much what I do. I
don't usually go anywhere. Just staying home, sleeping properly, and not setting
an alarm — for me, that's the vacation.
```

**점수를 버는 지점**
- `I don't even know why — it's not like I'm going to trade at eleven p.m.` → **자기 자신에 대한 논평.** IM에서는 거의 안 나오는 표현
- `which sounds a bit sad, but that's actually how I recover` → 예상 반응을 먼저 말하고 뒤집기
- `my mother would notice immediately if I skipped it` → 가정법 + 유머
- **마지막 문단이 `집에서 보내는 휴가` 문항의 답이 그대로 된다**

## Q3. 집과 관련해 기억에 남는 일 (과거 경험) ⭐

> *"Tell me about a memorable experience related to your home."*
> *"Has your home ever changed? Tell me what happened."*

```
Yeah, there's one thing that comes to mind. About five years ago, my family
renovated the inside of our apartment.

Like I mentioned, we'd been living there for a really long time, so everything
was old — the floors, the kitchen, the bathroom, all of it. And I'd lived in
that place since I was a kid, so I knew every corner of it. I could walk around
it in the dark without hitting anything.

The work took a few weeks. 〇〇〇〇〇  ← **아래 빈칸 채우기 참조**

And then I remember the day we came back. I opened the door and just stood
there for a second. It was the same address, the same building, exactly the same
floor plan. But it didn't feel like my house at all. Everything was bright,
everything was clean, and it even smelled completely new.

It honestly felt like moving into a new apartment without moving anywhere. I
remember walking around it really slowly, almost like a visitor.

What surprised me most was how much it changed my mood. I started spending a lot
more time at home after that. I hadn't realized how much that old, worn-out
space had been affecting me until it was gone.
```

**빈칸 하나만 채우면 완성이다** — 공사 몇 주 동안 어디서 지냈는지 한 문장.
사실대로 골라 쓰면 된다.

```
① 친척 집에서 지냈다  → "We stayed at a relative's place while it was going on."
② 근처에 잠깐 집을 얻었다 → "We rented a small place nearby for about a month."
③ 그냥 그 집에서 버텼다 → "We actually stayed in the apartment through most of
                            it, which was pretty rough — dust everywhere."
```

**점수를 버는 지점**

| 지점 | 효과 |
|---|---|
| `I could walk around it in the dark without hitting anything.` | **구체적 이미지.** 25년 거주를 한 문장으로 증명 |
| `same address, same building, exactly the same floor plan. But it didn't feel like my house at all.` | 짧게 끊어 나열 → 반전 |
| `moving into a new apartment without moving anywhere` | **문장 자체가 기억에 남는다.** 이런 표현 하나가 답변 전체 인상을 결정한다 |
| `almost like a visitor` | 비유 |
| `I hadn't realized how much… until it was gone.` | **과거완료 + 통찰.** IH 신호 |

## Q4. 예전과 지금 비교 (고난도 · 비교)

> *"Compare your home now with how it was before."*

**"예전 집 vs 지금 집"이 아니라 "리모델링 전 vs 후"로 푼다.** 같은 집인데 완전히 달라졌다는 게 훨씬 강한 답이다.

```
The biggest difference is that it feels much bigger now.

Before the renovation, the apartment was divided into a lot of small separate
spaces, and everything felt closed off and kind of dark. It was the same size,
but it felt cramped.

These days it's much more open, and there's a lot more light coming in. And the
funny thing is, nothing was actually added. The apartment didn't get any bigger.
They just changed how the space was arranged. But it genuinely feels like a
different size.

That's the part I still think about, honestly. I always assumed a bigger house
just meant more space. But it turns out how a space is arranged matters just as
much as how big it is.

The other thing that changed is how we use it. Back then I mostly stayed in my
room. Now I actually spend time in the living room too. So it changed how my
family lives in the house, not just how it looks.
```

**점수를 버는 지점**
- `Before the renovation… / These days…` → 시제 대비가 명확
- `nothing was actually added. The apartment didn't get any bigger.` → **부정으로 강조**
- `how a space is arranged matters just as much as how big it is` → **추상적 일반화.** 개인 경험에서 원리를 끌어내는 순간, 채점자는 문단 이상의 사고로 읽는다
- `it changed how my family lives in the house, not just how it looks` → 대비 마무리
