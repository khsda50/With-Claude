## Q1. 언제, 누구와, 얼마나 자주 보나요 (습관)

> *"When and how often do you watch movies? Who do you go with?"*

```
I'd say there are two completely different kinds of movie-going for me.

The first one is the one I actually care about. When an old movie I love gets
re-released in theaters, I go and see it — by myself, at night, on a weekend.
The most recent one was a Hong Kong film called Chungking Express.

People always ask me why I go alone, and why so late. It's because there's
almost nobody there. When the theater is basically empty, I can really focus,
and there's this... mood, I guess. It's kind of hard to explain, but a nearly
empty theater late at night feels completely different from a crowded one on a
Saturday afternoon.

The only rule I have is that it has to be close to home, because when the movie
ends, there's basically no public transportation left. So I always stick to the
theater near my place.

And then there's the other kind, which is just watching new releases with my
girlfriend on the weekend. That one's totally normal — we pick something that's
out, we go in the afternoon, we get popcorn. Same activity, completely different
experience.
```

**점수를 버는 지점**
- `there are two completely different kinds of…` → **두 경우로 쪼개기.** 답변 길이가 자동으로 두 배가 된다
- `People always ask me why I go alone` → 질문을 스스로 던지고 답하는 구조
- `there's this... mood, I guess. It's kind of hard to explain, but…` → **말을 더듬는 척하며 설명을 이어간다.** 유창성 연출로 최고급
- `when the movie ends, there's basically no public transportation left` → **실용적 이유.** 이런 구체성이 답변을 진짜로 만든다
- `Same activity, completely different experience.` → 짧게 끊어 마무리

## Q2. 좋아하는 영화 / 장르 (의견)

> *"What's your favorite movie? Why do you like it?"*

```
If I had to pick just one, it would be The Lord of the Rings. Easily.

There are a few reasons. The first one is the world. Someone built an entire
world — the history, the languages, the different peoples — and all of it holds
together. Nothing feels like it was made up on the spot. As soon as you're in
it, you believe it.

The second reason is the storytelling. It's a really long story, but it never
loses you. Every character has somewhere to go, and by the end, all of it
connects.

And then there's the filmmaking itself. Some of those scenes still look
incredible, and that movie is over twenty years old now. That's not easy to do.

In general, fantasy and science fiction are my favorites, and it's pretty much
for the same reason — I like being taken somewhere that doesn't exist.

The one thing I absolutely can't do is horror. I just can't. My girlfriend has
tried a few times, and it never goes well. I spend the whole movie looking at
the floor, so there's really no point.
```

**점수를 버는 지점**
- **이유 3개를 각각 전개** — 나열이 아니라 설명. 이유만 나열하면 IM, 전개하면 IH
- `Nothing feels like it was made up on the spot.` → 부정으로 강조
- `I like being taken somewhere that doesn't exist.` → **장르 취향을 한 문장으로 일반화.** 강한 문장
- `I spend the whole movie looking at the floor, so there's really no point.` → 유머로 마무리

## Q3. 기억에 남는 관람 경험 (과거 경험) ⭐ **이번 시험의 최고 카드**

> *"Tell me about a memorable experience you had watching a movie."*

**전부 과거형. 그리고 마지막에 Q1과 고리를 맞춘다.**

```
Okay, this one is a little embarrassing, but it's the one I always think of.

A while ago I went to see an old Korean movie called The Classic. It's from the
early two-thousands. And like I said, I went by myself, at night.

I knew it was supposed to be sad — I'd heard that before I went. But I really
wasn't prepared for the ending. In the last twenty minutes or so, something gets
revealed about the story, and suddenly everything you watched before that means
something completely different.

And I started crying. And I don't mean a few tears — I mean I was completely
gone. I was trying to be quiet about it, which honestly made it worse.

Then the movie ended, the lights came on, and I couldn't get up. I just sat
there in my seat, because I couldn't stop. I waited until everyone else had
left, and then I waited a little longer, and eventually I walked out.

The funny thing is, that's exactly why I go alone, at night. If the theater had
been full, or if I'd gone with someone, it would have been a completely
different experience. I probably would have held it in.

So yeah, it was embarrassing. But it's also kind of the reason I keep going
back.
```

**왜 이 답변이 강한가**

| 지점 | 효과 |
|---|---|
| `this one is a little embarrassing, but…` | **첫 문장부터 듣게 만든다** |
| `I knew it was supposed to be sad. But I really wasn't prepared for…` | 기대 vs 현실 |
| `I don't mean a few tears — I mean I was completely gone.` | **자기 수정으로 강조.** 원어민이 실제로 쓰는 방식 |
| `I was trying to be quiet about it, which honestly made it worse.` | 유머 |
| `the lights came on, and I couldn't get up` | **구체적 장면.** 이 한 줄이 답변 전체를 살린다 |
| `If the theater had been full, or if I'd gone with someone, it would have been a completely different experience.` | **가정법 과거완료.** IH 판정의 강한 신호 |
| `that's exactly why I go alone, at night` | **Q1의 습관과 고리를 맞춘다.** 답변이 하나의 완결된 이야기가 된다 |

> 이 마지막 고리가 이 답변의 진짜 무기다.
> 채점자 입장에서 "이 사람은 준비된 문장을 읊는 게 아니라 **자기 이야기를 하고 있다**"로 읽힌다.

## Q4. 영화관 vs 집에서 보기 (고난도 · 비교)

> *"Compare watching movies at home with watching them in a theater."*

```
They're really different for me, and I've actually gotten pretty strict about
it.

At home, on Netflix, I'll watch almost anything. If it's bad, I stop after
twenty minutes and put something else on. There's no cost to it — I can pause
it, check my phone, go make food, whatever I want.

But going to a theater is different, because it costs time and money, and I
can't undo it. So these days I only go for two kinds of movies: something I've
already seen and loved, or something with really good reviews. I don't take
chances anymore.

Some people would say that's boring, and maybe it is. But I'd rather watch a
movie I already know is great, on a big screen, with the sound, than gamble on
something new.

And I think that's the real difference. At home, I'm mostly just filling time.
In a theater, I'm actually there for the movie.
```

**점수를 버는 지점**
- `There's no cost to it — I can pause it, check my phone, go make food` → 나열로 리듬
- `I don't take chances anymore.` → 짧은 단정문. 긴 문장 사이에 넣으면 강해진다
- `Some people would say that's boring, and maybe it is. But…` → **반론을 먼저 인정하고 뒤집기.** IH~AL급 전개
- `At home, I'm mostly just filling time. In a theater, I'm actually there for the movie.` → **대구 마무리**

## 발음 주의

- `theater` [**씨**어터] — 첫소리가 th
- `genre` [**쟌**러] — g를 j처럼. 자신 없으면 `type of movie` 로 바꿔도 된다
- `Chungking Express` / `The Classic` — 한국식으로 말해도 전혀 문제없다. 제목은 원래 그렇다
- `re-released` [리-릴**리**스트] — 이번 스크립트의 핵심어. 20회 반복해 둘 것
